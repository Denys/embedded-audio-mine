**1. [reales/retromulator](https://github.com/reales/retromulator)** — stars 55; pushed 2026-09-27
> A standalone app and DAW plugin (AAX / VST3 / AU) that emulates classic hardware synthesizers, samplers, and keyboards by running their original firmware on cycle-accurate CPU and DSP emulators.

Content evidence: Matrix-1000 CEM3396 voice model implements four-sample BLEP/BLAMP corrections and a separate 2x halfband decimator; bundled SFZero/wxWidgets files are not counted as project hardware.
Sources: [source/matrixLib/cem3396.cpp](https://github.com/reales/retromulator/blob/c20db50773c13a79bd499c0334eb4b3e8f0104c0/source/matrixLib/cem3396.cpp); [source/matrixLib/halfband2x.h](https://github.com/reales/retromulator/blob/c20db50773c13a79bd499c0334eb4b3e8f0104c0/source/matrixLib/halfband2x.h).

Portability judgment: medium — Port only the oscillator correction and decimator subset; the full firmware/CPU emulator is not qualified for Cortex-M7. Build, CPU and memory checks remain NOT_RUN.

Reuse idea: Implement the four-sample BLEP/BLAMP reset in a small Daisy/Teensy oscillator and compare hard-sync aliasing before adopting 2x oversampling.

Repeat evidence: Re-entry after September 22: the new Matrix-1000 core and BLEP/BLAMP/2x decimation are substantive synthesis additions, not just a fresh push. [Inspected update](https://github.com/reales/retromulator/commit/698d9de53b72edfab3e503e068bd17c8f6cea1b0).

> Samplotron is an ESP32 hardware sampler for musicians and producers: play WAVs from SD, map samples to MIDI notes, tweak per-sample volume and shot/loop mode, and save live-ready setups on device.

Content evidence: The ESP32 sampler voice engine implements gain/fade/voice lifecycle; audio.h exposes a separate SD reader task and streaming-underrun diagnostics.

Reuse idea: Give SD reads a separate task, preload sample starts into RAM, and log underruns and maximum read latency before increasing polyphony.

Repeat evidence: Repeat after September 15: merged SD-streaming underrun fixes change practical sampler reuse; the pinned source exposes reader-task separation and diagnostics. [Inspected update](https://github.com/jakubthedeveloper/Samplotron/commit/19518f61320de08eb13321d70a0534ee36ccd3f5).
